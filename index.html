Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Who is eligible to select the "No Income" category?

Students dependent on parents/guardians, full-time college attendees, and registered unemployed youth seeking stipends or competitive exam scholarships can select the "No Income" option.

Q2: Is Aadhaar mandatory for checking schemes?

No. Aadhaar is completely optional for browsing and discovering schemes. However, it is required when officially submitting an application for DBT Direct Benefit Transfer verification.

Q3: How long does district verification take?

District verification typically takes 3 to 7 working days. Once the District Verification Officer approves your submission, status changes to "Approved" with remarks under "My Applications".

About YojanaSetu

Unified Public Digital Gateway for Central & State Welfare Delivery

YojanaSetu is a citizen-first digital bridge developed to connect every Indian citizen to legitimate Central and State Government welfare schemes without intermediaries. By organizing multi-tier eligibility rules across education, agriculture, women empowerment, housing, and social security, YojanaSetu eliminates information barriers and bureaucratic delays.

🎯

Citizen First Mission

Ensuring that students, farmers, rural women, and unemployed youth easily find government benefits matched to their exact financial standing and district residence.

⚡

100% Cashless DBT

Direct integration with the Aadhaar-enabled Public Financial Management System (PFMS) ensures benefits reach verified beneficiary bank accounts directly.

🛡️

District Level Transparency

Dedicated verification portals for District Magistrates and Nodal Officers provide real-time accountability and transparent approval tracking for every submitted application.

🇮🇳

Pan-India Coverage

Comprehensive repository of flagship Central schemes alongside state-specific initiatives across Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, and other states.
